Plinth ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the Plinth app, why we collect it, and how you can control it. By using Plinth, you agree to this policy.

Data We Collect

Account
  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Password (hashed)
  • Bio (optional)
  • Profile & cover photo URLs (optional)
Gaming Data
  • Achievements & trophies
  • Game library & completion
  • Platinum trophies
  • XP & level progression
  • Collection status per game
User Content
  • Game reviews & ratings
  • Favorite game showcase
  • Profile visibility settings
Technical
  • Session tokens (encrypted)
  • Security audit events (IP, login attempts)
  • Sync job status per platform

What we do NOT collect: location data, device contacts, camera or microphone access, biometric data, phone number, date of birth, or any advertising identifiers. Our Android app requests only the INTERNET permission.

Linked Gaming Platforms

When you link your gaming accounts, Plinth connects to the following platforms to sync your achievements and library. Platform credentials (tokens) are stored encrypted on our servers and are never shared with third parties.

⬡ PlayStation Network ⬡ Xbox / Microsoft ⬡ Steam / Valve

You can unlink any platform at any time from your profile settings. Unlinking removes your stored credentials immediately.

How We Use Your Data

  • Create and manage your Plinth account
  • Sync your gaming achievements, trophies, and progress from linked platforms
  • Display your gaming profile, collection, and portfolio to you and (optionally) other users
  • Calculate your XP, level, and gamification stats
  • Show your activity in the social feed and "Friends Activity" section
  • Secure your account (rate limiting, session management, audit logging)
  • Enable password reset and account recovery
  • Display affiliate offers for games you play (see Third Parties below)

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for targeted advertising. We do not build advertising profiles.

Third-Party Services

Gaming Platforms (Sony, Microsoft, Valve)

Connecting a gaming account requires authenticating with Sony (PlayStation), Microsoft (Xbox), or Valve (Steam). These connections are subject to each platform's own privacy policy. Plinth stores only the encrypted session token needed to sync your data — never your platform password.

Affiliate Partners

Plinth displays labeled affiliate links for game keys and accounts from the following marketplaces:

Instant Gaming — Affiliate link G2A — Affiliate marketplace

When you click an affiliate link, a randomly generated click ID and a one-way hashed version of your user ID (SHA-256, first 24 characters) are passed to the partner as a tracking parameter (subId). This allows commission attribution. No other personal data (email, name, IP) is shared with affiliate partners. Affiliate links are always clearly labeled in the app.

Security

Encrypted in transit
All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS).
Encrypted at rest
Platform session tokens are encrypted before being stored in our database.
Hashed passwords
Passwords are never stored in plaintext — only a secure one-way hash is kept.
Secure local storage
On-device session tokens use flutter_secure_storage (system keychain).
Rate limiting
Authentication endpoints are rate-limited to prevent brute force attacks.
Audit logging
Security-relevant events (login, password change, sessions revoked) are logged.

Guest Accounts

Plinth offers a 7-day guest trial. A guest account requires only a display name — no email address or password. Guest account data is automatically deleted after the 7-day trial period expires unless you convert to a full account by adding an email and password.

Guest accounts are subject to the same data practices described in this policy.

Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data (email, display name, bio, reviews, linked platform tokens, and collection data) is permanently deleted from our systems. Anonymized security audit logs may be retained for a short period for fraud prevention purposes.

Your Rights

  • Access — You can view all data in your profile at any time.
  • Correction — You can update your display name, bio, and avatar in profile settings.
  • Deletion — You can request full deletion of your account and data at any time.
  • Portability — Contact us to request an export of your data.
  • Unlinking — You can disconnect PlayStation, Xbox, or Steam at any time from your settings.
