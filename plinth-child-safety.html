Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 24, 2026

🛡️
Zero Tolerance for Child Exploitation Plinth has a strict zero-tolerance policy toward any content that sexualizes, exploits, grooms, or endangers minors. This page describes our standards and how to report violations.

About Plinth

Plinth is a social gaming app that allows users to track video game achievements and trophies across PlayStation, Xbox, and Steam, and share their gaming profiles with others. Users can publish reviews, build a game collection, and view friends' activity.

While Plinth is intended for users aged 13 and above, we recognize our responsibility to protect all users — especially minors — from harmful content and interactions.

Prohibited Content & Behavior

✕
Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) of any kind
✕
Grooming, soliciting, or predatory behavior toward minors
✕
Sexual content involving or targeting minors
✕
Content that exploits, endangers, or abuses children
✕
Sharing or distributing harmful content targeting minors
✕
Any attempt to collect personal data from children under 13

Violations of these standards will result in immediate account termination. Where legally required, we will report violations to the appropriate national and regional authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United States and equivalent bodies in other jurisdictions.

How to Report a Child Safety Concern

If you encounter any content or behavior that you believe violates child safety, you can report it directly to our team. We treat all child safety reports as the highest priority and will act within 24 hours of receiving a report.

When reporting, please include:

  • A description of the content or behavior
  • The username or profile link of the account involved (if applicable)
  • A screenshot or other evidence if available
Report a Child Safety Issue

Send your report to our designated child safety contact. Use the subject line "Child Safety Report" so we can prioritize your message.

vaycepro@gmail.com

You can also report content directly in the app by using the "Report" option on any profile or review.

Our Commitments

✓
We comply with all applicable laws regarding child safety, including COPPA, GDPR-K, and equivalent national regulations.
✓
We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
✓
We will report any discovered CSAM or child exploitation to NCMEC and relevant national authorities without delay.
✓
We provide an accessible in-app and email mechanism for users to report child safety concerns.
✓
Accounts found to be in violation of these standards are permanently banned and may be reported to law enforcement.
✓
We review and update these standards regularly to reflect best practices and legal requirements.

Enforcement

Upon receiving a child safety report, Plinth will:

  • Acknowledge the report within 24 hours
  • Review the reported content or account immediately
  • Remove any violating content and suspend or ban the offending account
  • Report to NCMEC or equivalent national authority if CSAM is identified
  • Cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations
